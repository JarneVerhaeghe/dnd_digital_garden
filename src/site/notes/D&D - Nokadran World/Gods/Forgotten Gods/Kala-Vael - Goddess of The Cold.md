# Kala-Vael - Goddess of The Cold
## Background
Kala-Vael was the Old Goddess of the biting frost, winter storms, glacial preservation, and the relentless march of the northern cold. Unlike Thalassa, whose rage was loud and crashing, Kala-Vael represented the quiet, disciplined cruelty of frost that claims the unprepared without malice or anger. Her primary quirk was her obsession with stillness and thermal purity: whenever she entered an area, all sound was dampened to a muffled whisper as if buried under thick snow, and she despised open flames so fiercely that no fire could be lit within a mile of her shrines. Before the Ascension Wars, northern settlements and travellers prayed to her not for warmth, but to beg her to march her blizzards past their valleys. Just at the start of the Ascension Wars, with the rise of Nalar the first, an unnaturally warm thaw briefly swept the northern reaches of Nokadran. When the winter frost returned the following season, Kala-Vael’s divine presence was gone, leaving the cold behind as a mere force of weather rather than the will of an armoured goddess. No record exists of where she went or what caused her disappearance.

She is often depicted as a tall, stoic warrior woman clad in frost-rimmed full plate mail, with long, flowing icy-blue hair that never tangles in the wind. In one hand she carries a heavy tower shield of solid glacial ice, and in the other, a spiked flanged mace that shatters whatever it freezes. Her symbol is a spiked mace and a shield crossed over a six-pointed snowflake.

Domains: Tempest, Nature, and War
Alignment: Lawful Neutral
