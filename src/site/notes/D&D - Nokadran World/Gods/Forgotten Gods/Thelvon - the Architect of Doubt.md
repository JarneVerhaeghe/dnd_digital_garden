# Thelvon - the Architect of Doubt
## Background
Thelvon was the Old God of psychology, manipulation, paranoia, and the fragile architecture of the mortal mind. Where Oteus sought pure truth and knowledge, Thelvon delighted in how easily certainty could be unravelled by a single misplaced word or a lingering suspicion. He never fought in battles or displayed raw destructive power; instead, he stood for the invisible strings of ulterior motives, gaslighting, and cognitive traps. Many historians in Nokadran suspect that Thelvon whispered the first seeds of distrust between the followers of Nalar and the defenders of the natural cycle, quietly setting the stage for the Ascension Wars. However, right as the conflict boiled over into open war, Thelvon vanished completely. His covert cults found their communion mirrors dark, and his whispers disappeared from the royal courts. Because of his deceptive nature, scholars still debate whether he perished at the start of the war or engineered his own disappearance to watch the world tear itself apart from the shadows.

He is depicted as a slender, cloaked figure wearing a polished silver mask split down the middle, one half smiling warmly, the other half blank and eyeless. His most unsettling quirk was that his voice always echoed inside a listener's mind a second before his lips moved, making mortals doubt whether the thought was spoken by him or born from their own darkest insecurities. He also compulsively mimicked the exact posture and breathing of whoever stood before him. His symbol is a silver hand holding a cracked mirror that reflects an empty shadow.

Domains: Trickery, Knowledge, and Order
Alignment: Lawful Evil
