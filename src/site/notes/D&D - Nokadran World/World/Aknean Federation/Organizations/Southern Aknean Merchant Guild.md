# Southern Aknean Merchant Guild
## Background
The Southern Aknean Merchant Guild is the dominant commercial powerhouse of [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/The Aknean Federation of the United races\|The Aknean Federation of the United races]]. Founded shortly after the religious revolution split the southern continent into the Federation west of the river and the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Nokari Coasts/Nokari Coasts\|Nokari Coasts]] to the east, the guild was born out of economic necessity. While their eastern rival, [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Nokari Coasts/Organizations/The Radiant Merchant Guild\|The Radiant Merchant Guild]] of the Nokari Coasts, operates under strict theocratic oversight where a defaulted loan is treated as a mortal sin against the Divine Couple, the Southern Aknean Merchant Guild champions secular capitalism, technological venture, and aggressive free-market innovation.

Headquartered across the southern trade hubs, with its wealthiest maritime seat in [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Antisea\|Antisea]] and its political lobby in [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Akartum - Capital\|Akartum - Capital]], the guild acts as the primary financier for the Federation's inventors, artificers, and explorers. Because the Aknean Federation is governed by the democratically elected [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/Great Council of The Aknean Federation\|Great Council of The Aknean Federation]], the guild cannot rule by decree. Instead, it exercises immense soft power by funding election campaigns, bankrolling the military contracts of the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/United Corps\|United Corps]], and co-sponsoring expeditions with the [[D&D - Nokadran World/Organizations/Scarlet Assembly\|Scarlet Assembly]] to uncover pre-Ascension technologies and magic stones.

## Structure & Operations
The guild is led by the **Board of Scales**, a council of seven master factors known as **Scale-Lords**, each overseeing a vital sector of Southern Aknean commerce:
1. **Maritime & Coastlines:** Based in [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Antisea\|Antisea]], managing ocean fleets, shipbuilding, and trade across the southern seas.
2. **Caravans & Desert Extraction:** Also operating out of [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Antisea\|Antisea]], funding silica mining, spice routes, and nomad toll-treaties.
3. **Artifice & Innovation:** Based in [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Akartum - Capital\|Akartum - Capital]], holding patents on new machinery, filtration systems, and magic-stone storage devices.
4. **Arms & Munitions:** Heavily present in garrison towns like [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Padurark\|Padurark]], managing smithies and supplying weapons and armour to the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/United Corps\|United Corps]].
5. **Textiles & Marsh Goods:** Overseeing silk production, marsh-herb exports, and dyes across the inland provinces.
6. **Bullion & Letters of Credit:** Operating the **Gold-Dust Exchanges** and issuing the Federation's standardized promissory cylinders.
7. **Border Customs:** Stationed along the eastern river border, negotiating tariffs and trade treaties with [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Nokari Coasts/Towns and Cities/Ortrann\|Ortrann]] and the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Nokari Coasts/Nokari Coasts\|Nokari Coasts]].

## Symbol
A pair of balanced brass scales set over a compass rose, encircled by an iron gear—representing the union of trade, exploration, and technological progress.
