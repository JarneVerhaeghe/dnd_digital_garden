# Antisea
## Background
### Origin of the Oasis Port
Long before the formation of [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/The Aknean Federation of the United races\|The Aknean Federation of the United races]], the site of Antisea was a sacred neutral ground known to the southern desert tribes as *An-Tisar* ("The Meeting of Waters"). Here, a massive subterranean freshwater spring surfaced at the edge of the coastal cliffs, allowing nomadic clans to water their herds and trade desert minerals with early coastal mariners. After the Ascension Wars and the signing of the Convention of Eldingborg, refugees, engineers, and merchants from across the southern lands settled permanently around the oasis, building great sandstone ziggurats, deep subterranean cisterns, and cyclopean harbour quays. When the religious revolution in the east split the southern nation into the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Nokari Coasts/Nokari Coasts\|Nokari Coasts]] and the Aknean Federation, Antisea became the Federation's most vital southern sea port and the primary maritime seat of the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/Southern Aknean Merchant Guild\|Southern Aknean Merchant Guild]].

### The Season of Dry Sails & Democratic Reform
For decades, Antisea's five-year municipal elections were dominated by wealthy guild magnates who treated the surrounding desert nomads as second-class outsiders, despite the tribes holding official Federation citizenship. Ten years ago, the Guild-backed mayor attempted to pass the *Cistern Privatization Act*, which would have metered the ancient oasis springs and levied a heavy water tariff on nomadic herdsmen and working-class dockers.

In response, **Nefari al-Sari**, a former nomad war-leader of the **Al-Sari Wind-Riders** who had studied civil law and engineering under a [[D&D - Nokadran World/Organizations/Scarlet Assembly\|Scarlet Assembly]] scholarship, united the seven major desert clans, the harbour unions, and the working-class residents of the Dust Ward. Rather than launching a bloody siege against the city walls, she used the Federation's own democratic laws and economic leverage against the Guild. She orchestrated the "Season of Dry Sails": a coordinated, legal strike where registered nomad caravaneers refused to guide Guild trade trains across the dunes, while dockworkers slowed harbour inspections to a crawl and corsairs choked the outer bay. Starved of trade and fresh oasis supplies, the Guild-backed administration collapsed within three months. At the subsequent five-year municipal election, Nefari registered thousands of nomadic and working-class citizens and won a historic landslide victory to become **High Consul** (Mayor) of Antisea.

### Governing for the Federation
Today, High Consul Nefari al-Sari governs Antisea in her second five-year term, answering to the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/Great Council of The Aknean Federation\|Great Council of The Aknean Federation]] in [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Akartum - Capital\|Akartum - Capital]]. Her administration is a delicate political balancing act. On one hand, she must ensure Antisea meets its federal tax quotas, supplies high-purity optical desert-glass for Aknean technology, and maintains the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/United Corps\|United Corps]] naval garrison. On the other hand, she enforces the **Civic Oasis Charter** (also known in the streets as the *Unwritten Oasis Pact*): public water remains free to all citizens, the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/Southern Aknean Merchant Guild\|Southern Aknean Merchant Guild]] is kept in check by the elected Municipal Senate, and the underworld syndicates are quietly tolerated in the shadows so long as they never poison the city's water or strike a foreign dignitary inside the Oasis Crown. Because the Guild constantly schemes to unseat her in the upcoming election, Nefari uses intelligence leaked by underworld informants to expose Guild corruption.

## Description
Antisea functions as the southern maritime gateway and major mercantile hub of [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/The Aknean Federation of the United races\|The Aknean Federation of the United races]], sitting on the sun-bleached coast where the turquoise sea meets the scorching golden sands of the Southern Aknean Desert. As a vital stronghold for the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/Southern Aknean Merchant Guild\|Southern Aknean Merchant Guild]], the city's architecture is a breathtaking blend of Old Egyptian desert grandeur and coastal resilience: towering, flat-roofed ziggurats, colonnaded civic plazas, and obelisks carved from yellow sandstone and limestone, accented with lapis lazuli tiles, hammered copper, and salt-bleached cedar canopies. 

The atmosphere is a sharp sensory contrast of dry desert heat and cool sea breeze, smelling of roasted spices, myrrh, warm sandstone, and sea brine, while colorful linen awnings flutter above the streets to shield citizens from the relentless sun. Every five years, matching the electoral cycle of the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/Great Council of The Aknean Federation\|Great Council of The Aknean Federation]], the citizens elect their **High Consul** alongside a Municipal Senate. While the surface of Antisea gleams with legitimate democratic trade and technological innovation—exporting desert glass, rare minerals, spices, and oasis silks—the city harbors a notorious dark underbelly. Beneath the sandstone plazas and inside the labyrinthine cliffside coves, smuggling syndicates, black-market slavers, and whispered assassin guilds take full advantage of the busy ports and frontier distance from [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Akartum - Capital\|Akartum - Capital]].

# City Breakdown
## The Sun-Terraces (The Upper City)
Built upon the elevated sandstone plateaus overlooking the bay, this section of the city is cooled by sea winds and houses the municipal government, wealthy guild magnates, nomadic envoys, and civic institutions.

### The Oasis Crown
Located at the highest plateau where the ancient subterranean spring surfaces, this is the democratic and administrative heart of Antisea.
- **The Look:** Grand, stepped sandstone civic buildings inspired by Old Egyptian temples, flanked by towering obelisks capped with polished electrum that reflect the desert sun. Lush public hanging gardens and palm-lined pools are fed by stone aqueducts.
- **The Function:** Houses the **Sun-Crest Rotunda** (City Hall), where **High Consul Nefari** and the elected Municipal Senate debate city ordinances, mediate between settled merchant lords and roaming desert clans, allocate federal tax revenue, and manage public water distribution.
- **The Vibe:** Regal, breezy, and heavily guarded by the **Oasis Civic Guard** (spear-wielding guards draped in white and gold linen) alongside a local detachment of the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/United Corps\|United Corps]].

### The Lapis Quarter
The affluent residential, diplomatic, and bureaucratic district sloping down from the Oasis Crown toward the harbor.
- **The Look:** Wide, swept avenues lined with limestone villas painted in vibrant ochre and deep blue, featuring cool sunken courtyards and carved sandstone wind-catchers (malqafs) on every roof.
- **The Function:** Home to the regional magnates of the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/Southern Aknean Merchant Guild\|Southern Aknean Merchant Guild]], foreign ambassadors, campaign offices for council candidates, and wealthy scholars of the [[D&D - Nokadran World/Organizations/Scarlet Assembly\|Scarlet Assembly]].
- **The Fantasy:** Floating "Mirage-Lanterns"—a public innovation funded by the municipal budget—drift along the colonnades at night, casting a cool, moonlit glow that wards off desert scorpions and heat-fatigue.

### The Pharos Bluff
The westernmost promontory of the upper cliffs, jutting out into the turbulent waters where coastal currents meet.
- **The Look:** A sheer, wave-battered sandstone cliff crowned by the **Obelisk of the Burning Eye**, a colossal pyramidal lighthouse enhanced by Aknean optics.
- **The Function:** Guides merchant galleons safely past the jagged sandbars and reefs while serving as a lookout post and coastal garrison for the [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Organizations/United Corps\|United Corps]] navy.

## The Central Hub
### The Grand Souk & The Amber Quay
The blistering, chaotic economic heart where desert caravans unload their packs directly onto ocean-going vessels.
- **The Amber Quay:** A massive crescent harbor built from giant blocks of cyclopean limestone. Dhow-rigged ships, barges, and foreign galleons jostle for space alongside stone piers shaded by crimson sails.
- **The Grand Souk:** A sprawling, multi-tiered bazaar covered by woven reed roofs and dyed canopies, creating a twilight maze of spice stalls, glassblowers, money-changers, and beast-pens.
- **The Reality:** While Federation customs officers and guild inspectors tax the main thoroughfares to collect federal tariffs for [[D&D - Nokadran World/World/Aknean Federation/Towns and Cities/Akartum - Capital\|Akartum - Capital]], a single wrong turn down a narrow sandstone alley leads to the "Whispering Stalls," where contraband, untaxed magic stones, and illicit poisons change hands with a hand-sign.

## The Lower Dunes & The Under-Coves (The Shadow City)
Where the eastern city walls crumble into the encroaching desert and the sea carves hollow caverns beneath the cliffs, municipal regulations give way to the law of coin and blade.

### The Jackal's Maze (The Dust Ward)
A sprawling, labyrinthine working-class district of mud-brick and sandstone tenements pressed against the eastern desert wall.
- **The Look:** Narrow, sunken streets half-buried in drifting sand, connected by rooftop plank-bridges, canvas tunnels, and subterranean cellars dug to escape the midday heat.
- **The Industry:** Home to camel-breeders, sandstone quarrymen, alchemists, and—hidden beneath legitimate bathhouses—the safe-houses of the **Sirocco Veil**, the city's most feared assassin guild.
- **The Population:** Working-class voters, desert nomads, mercenaries, debtors, and smugglers who use the shifting sand dunes outside the gates to sneak goods past customs checkpoints.

### The Sunken Grottos (Smuggler's Maw)
Beneath the Amber Quay and Pharos Bluff lies a honeycombed network of natural sea-caves and flooded ancient catacombs.
- **The Look:** Half-submerged sandstone pillars, ancient eroded statues from a pre-Ascension desert kingdom, and secret rope-docks hidden behind waterfalls of municipal drainage cisterns.
- **The Function:** At low tide, shallow-draft skiffs slip into these caves to offload black-market cargo, forbidden necromantic relics from the north, untaxed magic stones, and unmarked passengers.

## The Outskirts
### The Caravan Gate & The Glass Dunes
Located on the southern and eastern periphery, where paved Federation roads dissolve into the open desert waste.
- **The Caravan Gate:** A monumental pylon gate flanked by twin forty-foot statues of seated jackal-headed guardians, serving as the customs checkpoint, staging ground for desert expeditions, nomad tent-markets, and voter-registration post for nomadic citizens returning from the deep desert.
- **The Glass Dunes:** A stretch of silica-rich sand east of the city where lightning and intense solar-mirror arcane furnaces—a marvel of Aknean engineering—are used to smelt Antisea's famous desert-glass and crystal lenses.
