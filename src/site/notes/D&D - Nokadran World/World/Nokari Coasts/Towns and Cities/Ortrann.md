# Ortrann
## Description
Ortrann is a border city on the South-Western border of the Nokari coasts. It also counts as a military and economic stronghold governing all trade coming from that part of the Aknean federation. The city is built on a cliff. The bridge over the river is a bridge over a ravine. There is a natural harbor on the seaside. There a path up the cliff to the city. Originally this was a dwarven city in the past, carved in the mountains but with the war, there were more people. As a result it become a mix of various cultures. The waves are rough here. The culture is also mixed with a bit more libertarian in religious laws. The guild has equal power here to the church.
